Marketing Agent Now Hiring Full Time

Marketing Agent at Rézoomé

Location: Remote within the United States

Employment: Full-time, 40 hours per week

Salary: $55,000–$65,000 per year

About Us

Rézoomé helps job seekers expertly tailor their resumes to specific job listings. We help customers present relevant skills and accomplishments clearly while keeping their experience accurate. We are seeking a Marketing Agent to introduce our services to users and build relationships with our affiliated communities.

About the Role

As a Marketing agent, you will turn customer needs into promotional content and campaigns that generate paid resume-tailoring orders. Working with our developers, you will explain how the service works, coordinate outreach, and use results to improve our marketing.

What You Will Do

  • Create and schedule social posts, short videos, blog content, and email campaigns for students, career changers, and clients looking for work.
  • Maintain a monthly campaign calendar and coordinate copy, design, approvals, and launch dates.
  • Write clear landing page and promotional copy that explains our service without promising interviews or employment.
  • Support partnerships with college career centers, student groups, professional communities, and relevant creators.
  • Test messages and calls to action, gather customer feedback, and work with resume specialists to improve educational content.
  • Use customer examples only with permission and remove personal information before publication.

What We're Looking For

Required

  • 1-3 years of marketing experience or equivalent internships, coursework, freelance work, or personal projects.
  • Familiarity with social media and design tools such as Canva, Adobe Express, or GIMP.
  • Reliable organization and engagement on social media platforms.
  • A related degree and equivalent practical experience are valued.

Preferred

  • Experience promoting a service
  • Experience running small paid campaigns
  • Experience using a CRM
  • Experience creating short videos
  • Experience supporting career services
  • Responsible use of AI tools with careful fact-checking

Benefits

  • 3 weeks paid time off
  • Flexible scheduling
  • Health Coverage.
  • Professional development allowance
